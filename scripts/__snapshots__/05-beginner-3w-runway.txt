PROFILE 5 — BEGINNER · 3 weeks · insufficient runway (§7.6)
================================================================================================
READINESS PLAN — not a training plan (PRD §7.6)
3 week(s) · starts 2026-08-05 · races 2026-08-25

WHAT WE ARE TELLING THE ATHLETE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  · There isn't enough time to build race fitness safely before this date. Trying to would raise your injury risk without meaningfully improving your finish.

  · So this plan does something different: it gets you familiar with all eight stations, teaches you what running off a station feels like, and gets you to the start line fresh and knowing what to expect.

  · Start slower than feels right. The first run is the easiest one you'll do, and going out hard is the most common way a first race unravels.

  · Know the station order, bring your own kit for the farmers carry grip, and walk the venue before you start if you can.

  · It's worth asking whether a later race would serve you better. There's no prize for racing this one under-prepared, and a date 12 weeks out would let you arrive genuinely ready.

  · If you'd rather race this date, Relay or Doubles splits the work between you and a partner or team — much less total load, and the same event-day experience.

WHAT THIS PLAN DELIBERATELY WILL NOT DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  · No race simulations. A full simulation this close would cost you more in recovery than it gains you in readiness.
  · No new maximal loading. This isn't the window to find out what your heaviest sled feels like.
  · No increase over your current training volume. Holding steady is the goal.
